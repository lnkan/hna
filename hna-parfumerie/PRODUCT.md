# HNA Parfumerie

register: brand

## Product purpose
Storefront for HNA Parfumerie, a perfume retailer in Ankorondrano, Antananarivo (Madagascar), founded in 2024 by three cousins. It replaces a Linktree page plus a generic marketplace listing (clicodeal.mg). Orders are not paid online: every sale closes in a WhatsApp conversation, so the site's job is to make someone want a bottle and get them into WhatsApp with the order already written.

## Users
People in Madagascar arriving from the Instagram, TikTok or Facebook bio link, almost always on a phone, often on mobile data, usually in the evening while scrolling. They know the designer names (Dior, Gucci, Jean Paul Gaultier) and want to know three things fast: is it real, how much in ariary, how do I get it.

## Brand
- Voice words: warm, assured, hand-picked.
- Line used by the business: "Parfum de luxe à Madagascar".
- Logo: black square, white line-drawn bottle with an HN monogram, set in Caslon. Caslon is therefore the display face.
- House colour: rouge latérite, after the red earth that gives Madagascar the name "l'Île Rouge". One committed colour, in the way Hermès owns orange.
- Physical reference: mouillettes (paper perfume blotters) fanned in a hand.

## Anti-references
- Black and gold "luxury" templates.
- Marketplace grids with SKU codes as the headline information.
- A list of links that all say "Cliquez ici".

## Principles
1. WhatsApp is the checkout. Every product is one tap from a pre-written order.
2. Prices in ariary, always visible, never behind a click.
3. Light on data: no framework, lazy images, system-friendly motion.
4. Only claims the business already makes (authentic, delivery across Madagascar, about two weeks on order).
